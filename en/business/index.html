Do you cater for condominium owners’ meetings?

Yes. We offer coffee bars for condominium owners’ meetings and corporate events in Bogotá. Proposals are tailored to the date, duration, venue and number of guests.

How much does a coffee bar for events cost?

We prepare a quote based on guests, duration, menu and logistics. Send us these details to confirm availability and the total price.

Can I buy green or roasted coffee in bulk?

Yes. We offer green coffee and bulk roasted coffee. Lots, quantities, packaging and timing are agreed on WhatsApp, subject to availability.

What equipment do you use for microlot roasting?

We roast microlots with a Kaleido M10S. We review the green coffee, quantity and the profile you want before agreeing on the service.

What is the minimum order and delivery time?

They depend on the service and lot. Tell us what you need and how much, so we can confirm the applicable minimum, scheduling and delivery.
