Before we begin

Your questions.
Clear answers.

Which drinks does the coffee bar offer?

We usually serve cappuccino, americano and filter coffee. We can also add other drinks to suit your event; tell us which ones so we can define the menu and its price in your quote.

Do you bring the coffee bar to my venue?

Yes. We bring and staff the coffee bar at client venues and events in Bogotá, including condominium owners’ meetings, corporate gatherings and business celebrations. Share the venue so we can review access and setup space.

How is the coffee bar priced?

Your quote is based on the number of guests, service hours, selected drinks and venue logistics. We use these details to present the service scope and total price so you can decide before booking.

What information do I need to request a quote?

For a coffee bar, share the date and time, venue, approximate guest count and service duration. Add any drink preferences in the details. You can start with tentative information and fill in the rest during our conversation.

How far in advance should I book the coffee bar?

Book at least 5 days in advance. Share the date and time so we can check availability and organise the service. If you are still planning your event, you can enquire with a tentative date.

Do you sell coffee for roasting or ready to brew?

We offer both: unroasted green coffee for roasters and coffee projects, and bulk roasted coffee for offices, cafés and organisations. Tell us which you need, the quantity and whether you want a one-off purchase or regular supply.

Can you roast a microlot of my green coffee?

Yes. We roast your green coffee with our Kaleido M10S, with a minimum of 1 kg per service. Tell us the lot’s origin, processing method and quantity, as well as your brewing method or desired profile. Before receiving it, we agree on the roasting date, delivery and price.

Does submitting the form confirm a booking?

No. The form prepares an enquiry that opens in WhatsApp for you to review and send. We then confirm availability, scope and price with you. An enquiry alone does not reserve a date or confirm an order.
