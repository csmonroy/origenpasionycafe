Privacy

Last updated: 2 November 2026.

Who handles your information

Origen, Pasión y Café, in Bogotá, handles enquiries received through its contact channels. For questions about your information, corrections or deletion requests, contact contacto@origenpasionycafe.com.

Enquiries and bookings

The forms prepare a WhatsApp message in your browser. The website does not send or store those fields in its own database. Your enquiry is shared with us when you send the message in WhatsApp. We use the information received to reply, prepare proposals and arrange your order or experience.

WhatsApp, email and social networks operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Optional analytics

Only if you accept do we load Google Analytics to understand which pages are visited and which contact buttons are used. We record the page type, language, button placement and service category. A click is not treated as a sale or a confirmed booking.

Our analytics do not send form text, WhatsApp messages, names, email addresses, phone numbers or the residential address. We remove query parameters and fragments from the page address sent to Analytics. We do not enable advertising features or Google Signals.

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions and processes technical information about visits. We configure analytics cookies for up to 180 days. Google may process information outside Colombia. See Google’s privacy policy.

Your choice

We store your acceptance or rejection in this browser for 180 days. You can change it through “Privacy preferences” at the bottom of any page. Withdrawing permission stops future measurement and deletes Analytics cookies accessible from this website; it does not delete data already sent.

If you decline analytics, you can still view the catalogue and contact us normally. Your choice applies to this browser; on another device you can choose again.

Website operation

Cloudflare hosts and protects the website and may process technical data needed to serve pages and prevent abuse. We also use Google Fonts and some Unsplash images, whose servers receive the requests needed to display those resources.
