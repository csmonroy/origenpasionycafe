02 / Curiosity is enough

From tasting a cup
to understanding it.

You don’t need to know coffee terminology. We start with what you notice and the questions you bring, with approachable explanations and hands-on learning.

Recognise what you taste.

In a tasting, we explore aroma, acidity, sweetness and body. You learn to describe your impressions and recognise what you enjoy in a cup.

Understand your brewing.

In the brewing methods class, we work on grind size, ratios, water and extraction. The goal is to understand what to adjust when brewing at home.

Explore your own interests.

A personalised session starts with your interests, experience and equipment. We can discuss espresso, latte art or your favourite brewing method to agree on a focus.

Your curiosity.
Clear answers.

Do I need previous experience for a tasting?

No. We guide you step by step through aromas, acidity, sweetness and body. Curiosity is all you need to start.

How long does a coffee experience last?

The tasting lasts 2 hours and is designed for 4–6 people. The brewing methods class lasts 2–3 hours and the personalised session 2–4 hours. Both are designed for 1–4 people.

How much does a tasting or class cost?

Indicative prices start at COP $80,000 for a tasting, COP $160,000 for the brewing methods class and COP $220,000 for a personalised session. We confirm the total price of your plan on WhatsApp before booking.

Where do tastings and classes take place?

Experiences take place in Bogotá. We arrange the venue, date and availability directly with you when reviewing your enquiry.

Can I enquire about a different group size?

Yes. Tell us how many people would attend and what interests them. We’ll review availability and a suitable proposal before confirming.
