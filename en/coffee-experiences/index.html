01 / Specialty coffee tasting

Learn to read your cup.

For a different kind of shared experience and a chance to discover what you enjoy in coffee. No prior knowledge needed.

What you take away

You’ll be better able to describe what you taste and make more informed coffee choices.

What you’ll explore

  • Recognise aromas before and after tasting coffee.
  • Distinguish acidity, sweetness and body from intensity.
  • Connect what you notice with your flavour preferences.
How the session works

We start with how to observe and taste a cup. We explore its sensations with guidance, then put what you discovered into words.

02 / Brewing methods class

Give your recipe a purpose.

For home brewers who want to stop adjusting everything at random. You can start with the basics.

What you take away

You’ll leave with a recipe to practise and a way to decide what to change in your next brew.

What you’ll explore

  • Understand the relationship between coffee dose, water and grind size.
  • Observe how time and extraction change the cup.
  • Adjust one variable at a time to recognise its effect.
How the session works

We discuss how you brew, work through a recipe and review its adjustments. Tell us which brewer you use when you enquire so we can agree on the class focus.

From COP $160,0002–3 hours · 1–4 people

03 / Personalised experience

Your question sets the direction.

For a specific interest: espresso, latte art or your favourite brewing method. We agree on the scope with you before booking.

What you take away

The aim is to help you progress towards a specific goal, with recommendations you can apply to your brewing.

What you’ll explore

  • Define what you want to improve and your starting point.
  • Work on the variables and technique relevant to that goal.
  • Review your questions and the next steps for practice.
How the session works

When you enquire, tell us what interests you, your experience and the equipment you use. We use that information to agree on the topic, duration and session arrangements.

From COP $220,0002–4 hours · 1–4 people

Before your visit

A good plan,
with clear answers.

These details will help you prepare for your visit.

Do I need coffee knowledge or equipment?

You can start without prior knowledge. If you’d like to work with your own equipment, tell us what you use so we can arrange it before the session.

Where do tastings and classes take place?

We welcome you in Bogotá by confirmed appointment only. When arranging your experience, we share the location and arrival instructions.

Is the displayed price my booking total?

Published prices are reference prices starting at the amount shown. Before booking, we confirm the total with you based on the experience and group.

Can I enquire about a different group size?

Yes. Tell us how many people would attend and what interests them. We’ll review availability and a suitable proposal before confirming.

When is my experience confirmed?

Your WhatsApp enquiry is the first step. Your experience is confirmed once we agree on availability, location, total price and booking terms.
