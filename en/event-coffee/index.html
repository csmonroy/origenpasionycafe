How much does coffee service for an event cost?

We prepare a quote based on guest count, duration, menu and logistics. Send us those details on WhatsApp to discuss the scope and total price.

Can I book the coffee bar for a condominium meeting?

Yes. We serve condominium meetings in Bogotá. We agree on the service according to the venue, schedule and guest count.

Which drinks and items are included?

The menu and setup items are defined in your event proposal. Before confirming, we review what is included and the venue requirements with you.

How far in advance should I enquire?

Contact us once you have a tentative date. We’ll check availability and the preparation your event needs; sending an enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Do you also offer coffee experiences for groups?

We offer tastings and learning sessions. For a business group, tell us how many people would attend so we can discuss a proposal based on group size and the purpose of the gathering.
