05 / Your questions, answered

Your questions.
Clear answers.

A few answers to help you choose.

Do I need to know about coffee to join a tasting?

No. Curiosity is all you need. We guide you through aromas, acidity, sweetness and body, step by step.

Where do the experiences take place?

We are based in Bogotá, Colombia. When you contact us on WhatsApp, we confirm the location, date and availability of your chosen experience.

What is the difference between a tasting and a brewing class?

In a tasting, you explore coffee aromas and flavours for 2 hours. In a brewing class, you work on grind size, ratios, water and extraction for 2–3 hours.

Can I book for a group or an event?

Yes. Tastings are designed for 4–6 people; classes and personalised sessions for 1–4. For other group sizes or an event coffee bar, share your idea and we’ll discuss a proposal.

Why choose recently roasted coffee?

Coffee loses some of its aromas over time. Recent roasting helps preserve them so you can enjoy the character of each origin. We work with small batches and offer advice on storage, grinding and resting time for your brewing method. Ask about available coffees and arrange your order on WhatsApp.

How do I buy coffee for home?

Choose Blend de Casa, Bourbon Rosado or Bourbon Sidra. When you select “Order coffee”, the form keeps your choice. Bags contain 250 g: Blend de Casa costs COP $25,000; Bourbon Rosado and Bourbon Sidra cost COP $40,000 each. We confirm grind options, availability and delivery on WhatsApp.

Does sending the message confirm my booking?

No. It is an initial enquiry. Your booking or order is confirmed when we agree on availability, the total price and the details with you on WhatsApp.
